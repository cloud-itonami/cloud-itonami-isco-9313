# Business Model: Independent Construction Labour Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9313`
- ISCO-08: `9313`
- Occupation: Building Construction Labourers
- Social impact: worker-safety, local-jobs

## Customer

- general contractors
- property developers

## Offer

- labour crew staffing
- site preparation
- material handling

## Revenue

- per-crew day rate
- contract staffing fee

## Trust Controls

- no work in an unmarked hazard zone without governor gate
- safety-plan sign-off required before any crew dispatch
- task and safety records are auditable, not editable
